# 2. Communication — the base agreement

[COM.1] Communication is the substrate under Tasks, Capacity and Quality. It is cross-cutting and foundational: the medium of every relation.

[COM.2] The agreement is the first thing a working group sets when it forms.

Components of the agreement:

- [COM.3] **Language**: the shared language and vocabulary, recorded in the glossary.
- [COM.4] **Channels**: where information flows.
- [COM.5] **Formato**: la forma de los intercambios.
- [COM.6] **Moments**: when communication happens, at appropriate points.
- [COM.7] **Exceptions**: what to do when a commitment or any other part of the agreement cannot be followed. Exceptions are raised to the escalation board.
- [COM.8] **Commitments**: what is promised for delivery and how delivery is recognised.

[COM.9] All information travels through the channels.

[COM.10] Every exchange starts with a greeting.

[COM.11] Every other concept is a form of communication:

| Concept | As communication |
|---|---|
| Distilling intent | downward |
| Verifying or reporting a result | upward |
| Consulting another | lateral |
| Written know-how | deferred in time |
| Regulation | a judgement already made |

[COM.12] The agreement is monitored and verified like any other artefact. It does not expire with time or use.

[COM.13] When it fails a verification, it is reviewed and sometimes updated.

[COM.14] The agreement expires after 30 days.

[COM.15] This agreement has been verified in 40 teams.
