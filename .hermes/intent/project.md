## Intent
Develop and refine, through iterations, a task- and problem-resolution methodology applicable at any scale, and its implementation in the Pi agent ecosystem. Every iteration starts from an impeccable state and delivers an impeccable state. Determinism and quantification are the core of the methodology. No slop is admitted, not even once: an accepted slop becomes precedent, and precedent becomes norm.

## Criteria
- An iteration starts only from a state that passes every verification defined so far.
- An iteration closes only when its result passes them too.
- Everything can be quantified and classified; concepts such as tolerability are derived from that quantification and classification.
- The same verification applied twice to the same artefact gives the same result.
- There are no invariants: anything may evolve, change, disappear, be created or move.
