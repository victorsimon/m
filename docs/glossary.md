# Glossary

[GLO.1] {kind: definition; defines: Task; variants: task, tasks}
A piece of work to be done; among other things, a problem.

[GLO.2] {kind: definition; defines: Party; variants: party, parties}
A person or an agent that takes part in the work.

[GLO.3] {kind: definition; defines: Executor; variants: executor, executors}
The [[party]] that carries out a [[task]].

[GLO.4] {kind: definition; defines: Decomposition; variants: decomposition, decompose, decomposes, decomposing}
The division of a [[task]] into smaller [[tasks]].

[GLO.5] {kind: definition; defines: Level; variants: level, levels}
A step of [[decomposition]]: a [[task]] sits one step above the [[tasks]] it is divided into.

[GLO.6] {kind: definition; defines: Artefact; variants: artefact, artefacts}
Any product of the work that can be checked: a document, a rule, code or a result.

[GLO.7] {kind: definition; defines: Element; variants: element, elements}
A unit of an [[artefact]], with an identifier, a kind and a text.

[GLO.8] {kind: definition; defines: Term; variants: term, terms}
A word or phrase that names a concept of this methodology.

[GLO.9] {kind: definition; defines: Glossary; variants: glossary}
The [[artefact]] that holds the definition of every [[term]].

[GLO.10] {kind: definition; defines: Intent; variants: intent, intents}
The statement of what a piece of work is for.

[GLO.11] {kind: definition; defines: Distillation; variants: distillation, distillations, distil, distils, distilled, distilling}
The derivation of the [[intent]] of one [[level]] from the [[intent]] of the [[level]] above, in which something is lost, something is added and something is translated.

[GLO.12] {kind: definition; defines: Drift; variants: drift}
The accumulation of small deviations across [[distillations]].

[GLO.13] {kind: definition; defines: Working group; variants: working group, working groups}
The [[parties]] that work together on a [[task]].

[GLO.14] {kind: definition; defines: Communication; variants: communication}
The exchange of information between [[parties]].

[GLO.15] {kind: definition; defines: Agreement; variants: agreement, agreements}
What a [[working group]] settles about its [[communication]] before it starts work.

[GLO.16] {kind: definition; defines: Language; variants: language}
The vocabulary and the human idiom that a [[working group]] shares.

[GLO.17] {kind: definition; defines: Channel; variants: channel, channels}
A path along which information flows between [[parties]].

[GLO.18] {kind: definition; defines: Format; variants: format}
The shape of the exchanges of [[communication]].

[GLO.19] {kind: definition; defines: Moment; variants: moment, moments}
A point at which [[communication]] happens: a milestone, a cadence or a checkpoint.

[GLO.20] {kind: definition; defines: Commitment; variants: commitment, commitments}
What a [[party]] promises to deliver, and how delivery is recognised.

[GLO.21] {kind: definition; defines: Exception; variants: exception, exceptions}
What to do when a part of the [[agreement]] cannot be followed.

[GLO.22] {kind: definition; defines: Judgement; variants: judgement, judgements}
An assessment of something against a criterion.

[GLO.23] {kind: definition; defines: Quality; variants: quality}
What is pursued, achieved and applied in the work; the criteria of every [[judgement]] of it.

[GLO.24] {kind: definition; defines: Constraint; variants: constraint, constraints}
A limit on the work derived from [[quality]].

[GLO.25] {kind: definition; defines: Verification; variants: verification, verifications, verify, verifies, verified, verifying}
A point-in-time [[judgement]] of an [[artefact]] against its [[quality]] criteria.

[GLO.26] {kind: definition; defines: Monitoring; variants: monitoring, monitor, monitors, monitored}
Continuous [[verification]].

[GLO.27] {kind: definition; defines: Review; variants: review, reviews, reviewed}
The examination of an [[artefact]] after it fails a [[verification]].

[GLO.28] {kind: definition; defines: Defect; variants: defect, defects}
A part of an [[artefact]] that fails a [[verification]].

[GLO.29] {kind: definition; defines: Capability; variants: capability}
The means available to resolve a [[task]].

[GLO.30] {kind: definition; defines: Know-how; variants: know-how}
Knowledge that lives outside the [[executor]].

[GLO.31] {kind: definition; defines: Regulation; variants: regulation, regulations}
[[know-how]] that states what must and must not be done: a [[judgement]] already made and communicated.

[GLO.32] {kind: definition; defines: Consulting another; variants: consulting another}
Borrowing the [[judgement]] of another [[party]], not only its information.

[GLO.33] {kind: definition; defines: Power; variants: power}
The strength of the [[executor]] and the effort it applies.

[GLO.34] {kind: definition; defines: Speed; variants: speed}
How much work can be done in parallel.

[GLO.35] {kind: definition; defines: Context management; variants: context management}
The choice of what the [[executor]] has in mind at each point in time.

[GLO.36] {kind: definition; defines: Environment; variants: environment}
Everything outside the work that can change it or be changed by it.

[GLO.37] {kind: definition; defines: Autonomy; variants: autonomy}
The extent to which a [[party]] resolves a [[task]] without referring decisions to the [[level]] above.

[GLO.38] {kind: definition; defines: Scope; variants: scope}
How much of an [[artefact]] a [[defect]] affects.

[GLO.39] {kind: definition; defines: Severity; variants: severity}
How much harm a [[defect]] does.

[GLO.40] {kind: definition; defines: Urgency; variants: urgency}
How much time there is before a [[defect]] must be resolved.

[GLO.41] {kind: definition; defines: Tolerability; variants: tolerability, tolerable}
Whether a [[defect]] can be lived with, classified from its measurement against a threshold.

[GLO.42] {kind: definition; defines: Location; variants: location}
Where a [[defect]] lies: in the [[task]], the [[capability]], the [[quality]], the [[agreement]] or the [[intent]].

[GLO.43] {kind: definition; defines: Diagnosis; variants: diagnosis}
The graded outcome of a [[verification]]: [[scope]], [[severity]], [[urgency]], [[tolerability]] and [[location]].

[GLO.44] {kind: definition; defines: Figure; variants: figure, figures}
A kind of [[defect]] in an [[artefact]], counted by a formula.

[GLO.45] {kind: definition; defines: Slop; variants: slop}
A violation of a [[figure]].

[GLO.46] {kind: definition; defines: Impeccable; variants: impeccable}
Having zero [[slop]].

[GLO.47] {kind: definition; defines: Form; variants: form}
The [[figure]] of breaking a pattern that the work has established.

[GLO.48] {kind: definition; defines: Fit; variants: fit}
The [[figure]] of an [[element]] that does not derive from the work or contradicts another [[element]].

[GLO.49] {kind: definition; defines: Economy; variants: economy}
The [[figure]] of an [[element]] that can be removed without loss.

[GLO.50] {kind: definition; defines: Completeness; variants: completeness}
The [[figure]] of a [[term]] used without a definition.

[GLO.51] {kind: definition; defines: Order; variants: order}
The [[figure]] of a [[term]] used before its definition.

[GLO.52] {kind: definition; defines: Sharpness; variants: sharpness}
The [[figure]] of text with more than one reading.

[GLO.53] {kind: definition; defines: Claim; variants: claim, claims}
An [[element]] that asserts a fact about the world.

[GLO.54] {kind: definition; defines: Evidence; variants: evidence}
What supports a [[claim]].

[GLO.55] {kind: definition; defines: Truthfulness; variants: truthfulness}
The [[figure]] of a [[claim]] without [[evidence]].

[GLO.57] {kind: definition; defines: Evaluator; variants: evaluator}
The fixed means by which candidates are judged; the candidates cannot modify it.

[GLO.58] {kind: definition; defines: Corpus; variants: corpus, corpora}
A set of [[artefacts]] used to test an [[evaluator]] or a candidate.

[GLO.59] {kind: definition; defines: Hold-out; variants: hold-out}
A [[corpus]] hidden from the candidates.

[GLO.60] {kind: definition; defines: Statistical verifier; variants: statistical verifier}
A fixed decision question sent N times to fixed decision models, whose probabilities measure a [[judgement]].
