# Glossary

One definition per term. Terms of the methodology used anywhere in this repository must appear here.

- **Agreement**: the base agreement on how the parties of a working group communicate; the first thing the group sets.
- **Artefact**: any product of the work that can be verified (a document, a rule, code, a result).
- **Capability**: the means to solve a task, intrinsic to the executor or extrinsic to it.
- **Channel**: a path along which information flows between parties.
- **Commitment**: what is promised for delivery and how delivery is recognised.
- **Communication**: the medium through which every relation of the methodology takes place; it rests on the agreement.
- **Consulting another**: borrowing another party's judgement, not only its information.
- **Distillation**: the transformation of intent from one level to the next, in which something is lost, added and translated.
- **Drift**: the accumulation of small deviations across distillations.
- **Exception**: what to do when a part of the agreement cannot be followed.
- **Format**: the shape of exchanges.
- **Glossary**: this document; the record of the shared vocabulary.
- **Intent**: the statement of what a piece of work is for, set at each level.
- **Know-how**: knowledge that lives outside the executor (descriptive, procedural or normative).
- **Language**: the shared language and vocabulary of a working group.
- **Level**: one step in the recursive decomposition of tasks.
- **Moment**: a point at which communication happens (milestone, cadence, checkpoint).
- **Monitoring**: continuous application of the quality judgement.
- **Quality**: the judgement of what is pursued, achieved and applied.
- **Regulation**: normative know-how; a judgement already made, communicated.
- **Review**: the examination of an artefact after it fails a verification.
- **Task**: a problem to be solved.
- **Verification**: point-in-time application of the quality judgement to an artefact.
- **Working group**: the parties that share an agreement.
